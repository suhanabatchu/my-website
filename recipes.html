🍩 Brigadeiro

Ingredients

  • 1 can of condensed milk
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • Chocolate sprinkles for the outside

Method

  1. Add the condensed milk, cocoa powder, and butter to a saucepan.
  2. Cook it over medium heat, stirring constantly for about 10 minutes.
  3. When the mixture starts to thicken, remove from heat and let cool in the fridge for at least 2 hours.
  4. Roll into small balls and coat with chocolate sprinkles.
  5. Serve and enjoy!

🥞 Fluffy Pancakes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • ¾ cup milk
  • 1 tbsp melted butter

Method

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, and baking powder in a bowl.
  2. Whisk in the egg, milk, and melted butter.
  3. Pour small amounts onto a hot, greased pan.
  4. Cook until bubbles form, then flip.
  5. Cook for another minute and serve warm.

🍝 Arrabbiata Spaghetti

Ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp chili flakes
  • 1 can of chopped tomatoes
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Method

  1. Cook the spaghetti according to package instructions.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté the garlic.
  3. Add chili flakes and tomatoes.
  4. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Season with salt and pepper.
  6. Toss the sauce with the cooked spaghetti and serve.
